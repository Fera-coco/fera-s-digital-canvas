# Brightdoor Homes (concept)

A concept real estate website for a Lagos agency, designed and built by [Fera's Studio](https://ferasportfolio.vercel.app).

What makes it different from a template:

- **Total package upfront.** Every rental shows the full first-year cost (rent, service charge, agency, legal, caution) as a number and a bar, on the card and on the listing.
- **Title check for sales.** Each home for sale lists its title documents (C of O, survey plan, Governor's Consent, building approval) and their status.
- **Price map.** A schematic Lagos map with price pins that update with the filters.
- **Living here.** Power, water, security and rush-hour vs off-peak drive time on every home.
- **Free inspection booking.** Pick a day and time; the request lands in the owner dashboard.
- **Owner dashboard.** Enquiries with status, listings, and an add-listing form that works out the move-in total live.

Listings, prices and agents are samples. Photos from Unsplash.

Stack: Vite, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS. Data is local to the browser (demo).
